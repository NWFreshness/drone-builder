Drone Lab

Build it part by part. Learn why it flies. Then fly it, crash it, and try again for free.

1Build

Put each drone together in the real order, from frame to props. Click any part to learn what it does; See-through shows the wiring inside.

2Understand

"How it flies" spins the motors and shows differential thrust, torque cancellation and mixing on the model you just built.

3Fly

Real stick layout (Mode 2), Betaflight-style rates, motor lag, battery sag and crashes. Plug in your radio as a USB joystick to train on the real thing.

Works offline. Progress and settings stay in this browser. Keyboard: W/S throttle, A/D yaw, arrows pitch and roll, Enter arms.
